Eleph ("Eleph," "we," "us," or "our") provides connected monitoring technology designed to help users and their designated contacts monitor activity, inactivity, presence, and device status.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Eleph mobile application, connected devices, websites, software, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Eleph and which features are enabled.

Account Information

When you create or use an Eleph account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account identifiers
  • Authentication information
  • Account preferences and settings

Passwords and authentication credentials may be processed by third-party authentication infrastructure and are not necessarily stored directly by Eleph.

Eleph Device Information

When an Eleph monitoring device is connected to the Services, we may collect information including:

  • Unique device identifier
  • Device name
  • User-configured device location or room name
  • Sensor type
  • Device configuration
  • Software or firmware version
  • Connection status
  • Last communication or heartbeat time
  • Diagnostic and operational information

Activity, Presence, and Inactivity Information

Eleph devices may generate and transmit information about detected activity or presence.

This may include:

  • Presence or motion detection events
  • Date and time of detected activity
  • Last detected activity
  • Presence state
  • Periods of inactivity
  • Duration information
  • Sensor-generated status information

Eleph is designed to monitor activity or presence without recording video, photographs, or audio.

Alert and Monitoring Settings

We may collect settings necessary to provide monitoring functionality, including:

  • Inactivity thresholds
  • Caution or alert thresholds
  • Notification preferences
  • Device settings
  • Designated contacts or notification recipients

App and Technical Information

When you use Eleph, we may automatically receive technical information such as:

  • Device and operating system type
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Connection information
  • Crash reports
  • Error logs
  • Diagnostic information
  • Security-related events

During beta testing, we may collect additional diagnostic information to identify and correct software or hardware problems.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Eleph to:

  • Provide and operate the Services
  • Authenticate users
  • Associate Eleph devices with accounts
  • Display current and historical activity information
  • Calculate periods of inactivity
  • Determine whether a device appears online or offline
  • Generate alerts and notifications requested by users
  • Maintain device connectivity
  • Diagnose hardware, software, and network problems
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of Eleph
  • Develop new features
  • Protect our users, devices, and systems
  • Detect fraud, misuse, or security threats
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use activity or presence information for targeted advertising.

3. Information About Other Individuals

Eleph may allow a user to configure a device that monitors activity associated with another person.

If you use Eleph in connection with another individual, you are responsible for ensuring that you have any consent, authorization, or other lawful basis required to use the Services in that manner.

Eleph is not intended for covert or unlawful surveillance.
